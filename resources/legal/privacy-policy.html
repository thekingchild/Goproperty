Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data GoProperty collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and the rights you have, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission's General Application and Implementation Directive 2025.

Who is responsible

GoProperty is software that property agencies and landlords use to manage buildings, tenants, leases, bills and payments. For the details an agency or landlord keeps about its tenants and property owners, that agency or landlord is the data controller, and GoProperty processes the data on its behalf and on its instructions. For your own GoProperty account (your name, email, phone number and sign-in records), and for the businesses that subscribe to GoProperty, GoProperty is the data controller.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, mobile number, password (kept only as a one-way hash), two-factor and passkey settings, and a record of sign-ins (time, IP address, browser and approximate location).
  • What agencies and landlords record about tenants: name, phone number, email, address, occupation, emergency contact, the type and number of an identity document, leases, deposits, bills, payments, receipts, proofs of payment, maintenance requests and their photos, and posts in a building's group.
  • Businesses that subscribe: the business's name and address, its bank accounts for collecting rent, and for higher Transaction Tiers its registration documents and the details of its directors and compliance contact.
  • Messages we send for you: who an email, SMS or WhatsApp message went to, when, and whether it was delivered.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service an agency, landlord or tenant signed up for (a contract).
  • To keep financial records and meet anti-money-laundering and tax rules (a legal obligation).
  • To keep accounts secure, stop fraud, give support and keep the service working (legitimate interests). GoProperty support staff may view an account to give support and to monitor the service; every such session is limited in time and recorded.
  • To send product news by email, only if you choose to receive it (consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your notification settings).

Who else processes it

We use providers to run the service: hosting and file storage, email delivery, SMS delivery, WhatsApp (through the agency's own business account), Paystack (when an agency or GoProperty takes card payments) and encrypted backups. Some of them store data outside Nigeria; we use them only with the safeguards the Act requires. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Account and business data: while the account is in use. When a business cancels, its data is kept for 90 days (so it can come back or take a copy) and then deleted.
  • Financial records (bills, payments, receipts and invoices): as long as accounting and tax law require; if a person's data is erased, these keep their amounts, dates and numbers without the person.
  • The audit log: 7 years for money-related entries and 2 years for others. Sign-in records: 1 year. Backups: up to 12 months.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its use, to take it elsewhere, and to withdraw a consent you gave. If you are a tenant or a property owner, ask the agency or landlord that manages your building first: they hold your records. You can also write to our data protection officer (below). We answer within 30 days. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at any time.

Security and breaches

Data is sent over encrypted connections, identity and bank account numbers are encrypted when stored, staff must use two-factor authentication, and every change is written to a tamper-evident audit log. If a breach puts personal data at risk, we tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of learning of it, tell the agencies and landlords affected, and tell you directly when the risk to you is high.
