The service

GoProperty is software for managing buildings, units, tenants, leases, bills, payments, receipts, maintenance requests and building groups. The agency or landlord that subscribes (the customer) decides what is recorded about its tenants and owners and is responsible for it.

Money

GoProperty never receives, holds, splits or moves tenants' money. Subscriptions

Customers pay GoProperty a subscription by the number of units they manage, after a free trial, plus VAT, as shown on the pricing page. After cancellation the customer's data is kept for 90 days and then deleted; the customer may export it before then.
