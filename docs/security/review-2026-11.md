No critical or high finding is open. Every fixed finding has a test that fails without the fix.

**How severity was rated** (one rule each):

- **Critical:** an outsider or a customer user can read or change another organization's data, take over accounts at scale, run code on the server, or move money.
- **High:** a specific account can be taken over, a two-person, step-up or two-factor control can be bypassed, or a role can gain rights it does not have.
- **Medium:** a documented control is missing or weaker than the docs say, or an attack needs a second condition (a stolen session, a host serving any name, a third-party script).
- **Low:** limited to what the actor may already see or do, or defence in depth.
- **Info:** an observation; nothing needs to change.

## Policy coverage

- **Workspace (`/o/{organization}`, 99 routes):** every route runs `auth`, `verified` and `SetOrganizationContext` (membership, 404 otherwise); models bound from the URL are organization-scoped, so another organization's id is a 404. Every controller checks its policy; every Livewire component authorizes in `mount` and again in each action (record ids `#[Locked]`, reloaded through the scope, re-checked by the services with `Gate::forUser`). Ids a browser can choose (tenants, units, bills, assignees) are re-checked by the writers. Agents' building narrowing holds on lists and writes (SR-13 was a read).
- **Portals (`/portal`, `/owner`):** records are rebuilt on every request from the signed-in person's own tenancies (`PortalTenancies`) or portfolios (`OwnerPortfolios`), then checked with `viewAsTenant` / `viewAsOwner` inside the record's organization; no route binds a model. Owner portal pages refuse every Livewire call (`ReadOnlyOwnerPage`).
- **Console:** every page has `canAccess()` (checked again on every Livewire request), every resource a policy, every state-changing action `->authorize()` plus the service's own check; step-up and two-person approvals where PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md asks. Every page × every default role is walked by `tests/Feature/Console/StaffTwoFactorEnforcementTest.php`.
- **Public routes:** each one has a reason and a limit (the list in `RouteCoverageTest`): tokens are hashed, one use and expiring; signed links expire (media 15 min, impersonation hand-off 60 s, compliance documents 5 min, exports and backups 15 min, online payment 12 h, email verification 60 min); webhooks prove the caller by HMAC or a secret token; the installer is 404 once installed.
- **Impersonation:** blocked actions in all four places (HTTP, Livewire calls, permissions, policies); since SR-03 also any check outside the session's organization.

## Other classes checked

- **XSS:** all 34 `{!! !!}` traced: escaped values, constants, the allowlist cleaners (`App\Support\SafeHtml` for staff-written site text, `Posts\RichText` for group posts, applied on write and on read), CommonMark with `html_input=escape` for email templates. Templates are data with whitelisted placeholders only (AGENT.md § 3 rule 10). Clean.
- **SQL:** every raw fragment uses constants or bindings; sort columns are fixed; `like` searches escaped (SR-24 aside). Clean.
- **Mass assignment:** no `$guarded = []`, no `fill($request->all())`; status and tier columns written only with explicit arrays by their services. Clean.
- **Redirects:** `intended` is only set by Laravel's guest redirect or the app's own routes; `away()` only to signed media links and Paystack's checkout URL. Clean (SR-01 was the host).
- **SSRF:** every outbound URL comes from config or a fixed host (Paystack, GoText, WhatsApp, GitHub, Turnstile, Slack's `hooks.slack.com` only); dompdf fetches nothing remote. SR-30 aside, clean.
- **Files and processes:** no `exec`, `eval` or process calls; `unserialize` only of the app's own cache with `allowed_classes`; zip reading streams one named entry (no zip-slip); generated names only. Clean.
- **CSRF:** exceptions only for the four webhook paths, which prove the caller by signature or token. Clean.
- **Logs and debug:** phones masked, no tokens or bodies with personal data, no `dd`/`dump`, no debug routes, `APP_DEBUG` off by default and written off by the installer. SR-35 aside, clean.
