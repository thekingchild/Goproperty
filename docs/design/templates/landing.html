    FAQ

    Questions agencies ask us first.

    Something else? Talk to our team.

    Does GoProperty hold my tenants' money?

    No. Tenants pay into your own bank account or your own Paystack account. GoProperty matches and records the payment and issues the receipt — it never receives, holds or splits rent.

    Can I bring my existing tenants and running leases?

    Yes. Import owners, properties, units, tenants, ongoing leases with their "paid until" dates and opening balances from Excel or CSV. A dry run shows every row before anything is saved, and imported leases are never back-billed.

    How is the price worked out?

    By the number of units (blocks/apartments) you manage: a base price that includes some units, plus a fee for each extra unit. Pay monthly, or yearly and get two months free. VAT is added at 7.5%.

    What happens when the free trial ends?

    Pick a plan and pay by card (Paystack) or bank transfer. Your data stays exactly as you left it.

    Can landlords and tenants log in too?

    Yes, with the same login page. Landlords see read-only statements for their buildings; tenants see their bills, receipts and tickets — across every agency they rent from.

    What are Transaction Tiers?

    Limits on how much money an agency manages each year. Tier 1 is active straight away; higher tiers need your CAC and SCUML documents. We'll tell you before you get close to your limit.
