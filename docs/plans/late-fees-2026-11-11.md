# Late fees on overdue rent (post-launch backlog item 2, task PL-02)

The first part of [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md § 7](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) item 2. Started 2026-11-11 at the product owner's request ("Build late fees"), while hosting is not chosen yet. The quit notice the item also names is a separate, later task: its legal wording differs by state and needs a lawyer's text.

## Decided by the product owner (2026-11-11)

1. **How much:** a percentage of what is still unpaid on the rent bill when the grace period ends, charged **once**, with an optional naira cap.
2. **Who keeps it:** the landlord, like rent: on the owner statement, less the agency's commission (owner statements already count every bill but utilities as rent; BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 7.9).
3. **Which bills:** rent bills only. Utilities, other charges and imported opening balances never get a fee.
4. **Quit notice:** later, as its own task.

## Defaults taken (for the product owner to confirm)

1. **Its own module,** `Modules/LateFees` (ARCHITECTURE.md § 8 rule 5). It never reads `bills`: Billing publishes `Contracts\LateFeeBills` (rent bills due for a fee; issue one) and a new bill type, `late_fee` ("Late fee").
2. **Off until the agency switches it on,** in Settings → Late fees (owners and administrators, like Rent payments; not while GoProperty staff impersonate; read-only while suspended). Defaults when switched on: **5 %** after **7 days**, no cap. Rate 0.01–50 %, grace 1–90 days, cap ₦1–₦100,000,000 or none.
3. **When:** every day at 01:20 Lagos, after bills are marked overdue (01:00), on the `billing` queue: each open rent bill whose due date is at least the grace days ago (due 1 March, 7 days: charged on 8 March), that is not void and was not imported. The percentage applies to what is unpaid that day, rounded to the kobo; a fee of zero is not charged.
4. **Once per rent bill, ever:** the module's `late_fees` table holds one row per rent bill (unique), written in the same transaction as the fee bill, so a re-run or two runs at once charge nothing twice (AGENT.md § 3 rule 6). Switching late fees off and on again never charges a bill a second time.
5. **The fee is its own bill** (an issued bill cannot change): type `late_fee`, the same tenant, lease, building and unit as the rent bill, due the day it is charged, one line "Late fee" with "5 % of ₦250,000 unpaid on INV-…, due 01/03/2027". Numbered, referenced, printed and paid like any bill; the tenant's credit is applied to it as to any new bill; overdue reminders cover it like any other bill; no new message type.
6. **Waiving:** void the fee bill (Bills → the bill → Void, with a reason; refused once anything is paid, as for every bill). A voided fee is never charged again.
7. **No per-lease setting yet:** the agency setting applies to every lease. A lease that should never be charged can be added later if agencies ask.
8. **All plans:** no plan gating.
9. **Audited:** `late_fee.charged` (log `late_fees`) with the bill ids, the rate, the base and the fee; settings changes through the settings store's own audit.
10. **The settings page reminds the agency** to charge late fees only where its tenancy agreements provide for them.
