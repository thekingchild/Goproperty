# P05 — Reminders, notifications, email templates & WhatsApp: waves and defaults

Rules: [ARCHITECTURE.md § 6.7](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) (reminder engine, ladders, channels, WhatsApp), § 7 (scheduler), § 8 (modules talk through contracts); [DATABASE_SCHEMA.md § 7](../../DATABASE_SCHEMA.md) (`reminders`, `notification_deliveries`, WhatsApp tables, `sms_credit_entries`); [PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md § 5](../../PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md) (email templates); [sms-provider-2026-10-29.md](sms-provider-2026-10-29.md) (GoText, GoProperty-owned SMS wording, never promotional); [DATA_IMPORT.md § 3](../../DATA_IMPORT.md) (imported tenants hear nothing until invited); AGENT.md § 3 rules 3 (dates through the cycle calculator), 6 (idempotency) and 10 (templates are data).

## Waves

- **Wave 1: P05-01 and P05-04 in parallel.**
  - P05-01: a new module `Modules\Reminders` with the `reminders` table, the four ladders (rent, utilities, lease renewal, overdue), the hourly dispatch job and the email and in-app messages. It reads bills, leases and tenants through new contracts of Billing, Leasing and Tenants.
  - P05-04: the email template system in the console. Independent of P05-01; once both are in, P05-01's wording moves into templates (the messages are already built from plain placeholder values).
- **Wave 2: P05-02** (SMS through GoText, `notification_deliveries`, channel preferences). It adds the `sms` channel to P05-01's engine. Built against a `log` driver until the GoText test account (PRE-03) and GoText's answers (sms-provider note, "To confirm with GoText") arrive.
- **Wave 3: P05-03, P05-05 and P05-06 in parallel.** SMS credits and the delivery health dashboard need P05-02's delivery log; WhatsApp needs the delivery log and the per-event channel order.

## Defaults for the open questions (orchestrator, 2026-10-29; for the product owner to confirm)

### P05-01 — the reminder engine

1. **Ladders** (ARCHITECTURE.md § 6.7 defaults), counted in Lagos calendar days from the bill's due date (T−n before, +n after) or the lease's end date:
   - rent due: T−30, T−14, T−7, T−3, T−1, T−0;
   - utility due: T−3, T−0;
   - overdue (every bill type still owing): +1, +7, +14;
   - lease renewal (to staff): T−60, T−30, T−7 before the lease's last day.

   They are the same for every organization in P05-01. A settings page to switch a ladder off or pick its days (with the "days to rent due" preview of UX_DESIGN.md § 4, section 6) is a follow-up, proposed as **P05-07**.
2. **Short cycles:** a "due" rung is used only when it is at most half of the bill's period, so every month gets the same rungs whatever its length. Weekly rent gets T−3, T−1 and T−0. Monthly rent gets T−14 and closer. Quarterly and longer get the whole ladder. A bill whose period is shorter than 7 days (daily rent) gets no reminders at all, overdue included: one a day would be noise, and the agency sees it in the overdue list.
3. **Before the bill exists.** With the default 7-day lead, rent's T−30 and T−14 rungs come before the bill is issued. Those reminders are about the lease's next period: amount, period and due date, with no pay link. Once the bill exists, reminders give its number, the amount still owed, the payment reference and the pay link. Each rung goes once per lease period, whether it was worked out from the lease or from the bill.
4. **No separate "bill issued" message.** The first rung on or after the bill's issue day is the tenant's first message about it. With the defaults the bill is issued on T−7, the day of the T−7 rung.
5. **Exact days, no catch-up.** A rung is sent on its own day only. A day on which the scheduler never ran is not made up later, so tenants never get a burst of stale reminders; the next rung still goes. A bill issued late (a manual bill, a late meter reading) starts at the next rung. Reminders are worked out only from the import's commit date forwards (DATA_IMPORT.md § 3); nothing is sent for dates in the past.
6. **When they go.** The job runs hourly. It plans the day's reminders from 00:00 Lagos and sends them from **08:00 Lagos** (no messages at night), so a run that fails is retried by the next hour's run. A reminder not sent by the end of its day is recorded as skipped (`missed`).
7. **Checked again just before sending.** A reminder is skipped, with the reason recorded, when:
   - the bill was paid in full or voided;
   - the lease ended, was terminated, or (renewal alerts) has a drafted renewal;
   - the tenant was archived or can no longer be reached.
8. **Tenant channels in P05-01:** email (the portal account's address, else the tenant record's) and in-app for a tenant with a portal account. Each channel is its own reminder row, so a retry never sends a channel twice. Phone-only tenants get nothing until P05-02 (SMS) and P05-06 (WhatsApp).
9. **Imported tenants** get no reminders until the agency has invited them to the portal (any channel) or they have a portal account (DATA_IMPORT.md § 3: "lets the tenant know nothing until Invite tenants now").
10. **Renewal alerts go to staff:** the agents assigned to the lease's building who hold `leases.manage`; when it has none, the organization's owners and administrators. In the app and by email, linked to the lease page. They are skipped when a renewal is already drafted, and for a rung that falls before the lease starts (a short lease). An auto-renewing lease is still alerted ("it rolls on by itself unless you change it"). The tenant and the building's owner get no renewal alerts in P05-01.
11. **Which organizations:** every organization except cancelled ones. Tenants of a suspended organization still get bill reminders: their bills still stand, and the portal still takes payment proofs. Renewal alerts are skipped while the organization is suspended, since its members cannot renew anything.
12. **Tenants who moved with their building** (P03-14, P04-04): their bills stay with the organization that issued them, and that organization keeps reminding them about those bills.
13. **Wording and branding:** plain text from plain values (never Blade), in the agency's name like ticket emails (sender "Adeyemi Realty via GoProperty", replies to the agency's email, BRANDING_DOCUMENTS.md § 1). The link is the portal's bill page for a tenant with an account, else the public pay page `/pay/{reference}` once the bill exists.

### Later P05 tasks (to confirm before each one starts)

14. **SMS rungs (P05-02):** to save credits, SMS goes only on rent T−7 and T−1, utility T−0 and overdue +1 and +7. Email and in-app go on every rung.
15. **SMS delivery status (P05-02):** a queued job polls `GET /sms/{uid}` until a final status. *Confirmed (product owner, 2026-10-30):* GoText gives delivery reports through that endpoint, not a webhook. *As built (2026-10-30):* checks 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 and 12 hours after sending; still unknown after that, the message stays `sent`.
16. **Email templates (P05-04):** seeded from P05-01's wording (one template per reminder type and rung kind). The console's `content-manager` role edits them; agencies do not edit wording, only their branding. *As built (2026-10-30):* six reminder templates (`reminder.rent_due`, `rent_upcoming`, `bill_due`, `bill_overdue`, `lease_ending`, `lease_ending_auto_renew`) with P05-01's wording unchanged; rent and other bills share the overdue template, as P05-01's wording did. Bounce and complaint webhooks for Postmark and Mailgun; SES (through SNS) is a follow-up.

### Defaults taken in P05-02 (orchestrator, 2026-10-30; for the product owner to confirm)

17. **"Verified number" is the portal account's.** Phone verification lives on the account (`users.phone_verified_at`, P03-01b), so only a tenant with a portal account whose number is verified is texted, at that number. A tenant record without an account is never texted, even when the agency typed a number.
18. **At most once.** A send whose outcome is unknown (the job died while calling GoText) is marked failed, never retried: a duplicate costs a credit and confuses the tenant. Other failures retry after 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then the delivery is `failed`.
19. **Real SMS only in production** (`SMS_DRIVER=gotext` and a token). Staging and local use the log driver until GoText's test mode is confirmed.
20. **Plain GSM text.** SMS wording uses "NGN" instead of ₦ and drops accents, so 160 characters fit one page (one non-GSM character would cut it to 70).
21. **Preferences.** An account may turn reminder emails and SMS off (Settings → Notifications); in-app reminders, security and transactional messages stay on. Staff may turn off renewal alert emails.
22. **SMS link.** The SMS links to the public pay page (`/pay/{reference}`) for every tenant, shorter than the portal's bill page.

### Defaults taken in P05-06 (WhatsApp; 2026-10-30; for the product owner to confirm)

23. **Who gets it.** WhatsApp is used only on the Growth plan and above (`services.whatsapp.plans` against `organizations.signup_plan_key` until P06's subscriptions) **and** with the organization's own working connection. Any other organization is reminded and invited exactly as before.
24. **Credentials: owners only.** The orchestrator suggested owners and administrators; ARCHITECTURE.md § 6.7 says the credentials are "editable only by the org-owner", so `whatsapp.connection` (connect, check, verify token, disconnect) is the org-owner's, blocked while staff impersonate. Administrators open the page (no token shown, not even masked), sync templates and map messages (`whatsapp.messages`).
25. **Webhook address.** Organizations have no ULID column, so each connection gets its own (`webhook_ulid`): `/webhooks/whatsapp/{ulid}`. GET answers Meta's challenge with the connection's verify token; POST needs `X-Hub-Signature-256` with the app secret; a missing or wrong signature gets 401 and nothing in the call is read or recorded.
26. **Statuses.** `sent` → `delivered` → `read`, never backwards. `read` is kept as `delivered` with provider status `read` (the status enum is SMS's and P05-03 is changing that area). Meta's `failed` after it took the message → `undelivered`; Meta refusing the send → `failed`.
27. **Template sync** on "Sync from Meta" and daily at 05:20 Lagos (before the 08:00 reminders). A template that is no longer approved (paused, rejected, disabled, pending again, deleted at Meta — kept as `disabled`), no longer utility, or whose variables changed switches its mappings off on that sync, with the reason shown. A template approved again is **not** switched back on by itself: the agency does it.
28. **Only utility templates** may be mapped (never marketing or authentication).
29. **Events.** One per reminder kind and rung (`reminder.rent_due.t7`; `reminder.rent_upcoming.t30` for rent before its bill exists, whose values differ; `reminder.utility_due.t0`; `reminder.bill_overdue.p1`), plus the messages that already used the WhatsApp contract (tenant and owner invitations, the sign-in link, the four payment notices). "Bill issued", ticket updates and tenant renewal notices have no message to map yet (P05-01 default 4; tickets tell parties by email and in the app).
30. **Channel order** is about the phone channels only; email and in-app reminders go as before. A reminder on an SMS rung may be "WhatsApp, then SMS" (the default offered), "WhatsApp only" or "SMS, then WhatsApp"; any other rung and every other event is WhatsApp only (invitations and notices never use SMS, P03 decision 3). The first channel that can carry the message is used, and at most one phone message goes per rung: "WhatsApp only" sends no SMS even when WhatsApp fails.
31. **Fallback.** WhatsApp is skipped for the next channel when the plan or connection does not allow it, the event has no active mapping, the tenant has not opted in (or changed number, or opted out), a mapped value is missing, or Meta refuses the send. A message Meta took and **later reports failed** falls back to SMS **on the reminder's own Lagos day only** (the no-catch-up rule of default 5); reported later, nothing more is sent. A call that broke off (unknown outcome) is not sent again by WhatsApp and also falls back, accepting a rare double over silence. SMS fallback goes through `SmsOutbox::queue()`, so SMS rules (verified number, SMS rungs, preferences, credits) still apply.
32. **Opt-in (Meta policy).** Reminders go by WhatsApp only to a tenant with an open opt-in at a number that is still theirs (the portal account's number, else the tenant record's). The tenant opts in on Settings → Notifications (a verified number only) or a member records consent given in person on the tenant's record (`tenants.portal_access`); either may record an opt-out, which applies at once. An opt-out also stops invitations and notices to that number. Invitations, sign-in links and payment notices otherwise need no opt-in: a member sends the first two on purpose, the notices answer the tenant's own payment. *To confirm with the product owner* (Meta's policy may call for consent there too).
33. **Phone-only tenants** without a portal account can get WhatsApp reminders once the agency records their consent (still never SMS: their number is not a verified account's, default 17).
34. **Real Meta calls only in production** with `WHATSAPP_DRIVER=cloud`; elsewhere a log transport (checks accept, a sync fetches nothing and keeps what is saved, sends are logged). The default driver stays `log`, so production must set `WHATSAPP_DRIVER=cloud`.
35. **Pay links in URL buttons.** A template's URL button already holds the fixed start of the address (`https://…/pay/{{1}}`); when the mapped value (the pay link) begins with it, only the rest is sent.

### Defaults taken in P05-03 (orchestrator, 2026-10-30; for the product owner to confirm)

36. **One credit = one SMS page, taken before GoText is called.** `SendSms` takes it in the transaction that claims the delivery, under a lock on the organization's balance row, so parallel sends never take the balance below zero (`tests/Drill/SmsCreditDrillTest.php`). A retried job takes nothing again. Every SMS is one GSM page today (default 20), so each takes one credit whatever GoText reports afterwards.
37. **At zero nothing is sent:** the delivery ends `no_credit` ("Not sent: no SMS credit"), GoText is never called and it is not sent later when credits arrive (a reminder is about its day). Email and in-app reminders carry on (separate reminder rows). `no_credit` is not counted as a provider failure in the console's delivery log badge.
38. **Credit given back** (an `adjustment` entry linked to the delivery) only when GoText refused the message outright on the last attempt and no attempt's outcome was unknown (GoText could not be reached or did not answer: `notification_deliveries.outcome_unknown`), nor for an attempt that died mid-send. An allowance credit comes back only within the month it was taken.
39. **Monthly allowance** from the plan (`config/plans.php` `sms_credits`: Starter 100, Growth 500, Scale 2,000; **Enterprise 2,000** until finance-admin sets a custom allowance for that customer in the console); an organization with no plan chosen yet gets Starter's. Granted for each Lagos month by the hourly `sms-credits-allowance` job (due just after midnight on the 1st; a missed run is made up within the hour), by the first credit change of the month, at signup (the hook that exists: the organization step; P06-01 has no subscriptions yet) and when the signup wizard's plan step picks a bigger plan (the difference, at once). A smaller plan or allowance applies from the next month; the allowance is never prorated. Every organization except cancelled ones (suspended ones too: their tenants still get reminders, default 11).
40. **Expiry and order:** last month's unused allowance expires (`expiry`) when the new month's is granted; purchased (and staff-added) credits never expire; sending takes allowance credits first.
41. **Told at 20 % and at zero:** owners and administrators, in the app and by email (a phone-only account in the app only), once a month per threshold, linked to Settings → SMS credits. "20 %" is the whole balance (allowance left + purchased) against the month's allowance; going straight to zero sends only the zero notice. Wording: GoProperty templates `sms_credits.low` / `sms_credits.empty` (transactional, platform branding), editable by `content-manager`.
42. **Who sees and buys:** a new permission `sms_credits.view` (owners, administrators; kept while suspended and in support sessions: the ledger holds no contact details, numbers are masked). Buying is paying GoProperty, so it needs `organization.billing` (owners only, as administrators have "no billing responsibility"), never while GoProperty staff impersonate, and **not while the organization is suspended** (the workspace is read-only then; only the subscription may be paid — BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 2 sanctions nothing else).
43. **Bundles and price:** ₦6 per credit before VAT (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 1.2's example), bundles of 500, 1,000, 2,500 and 5,000 credits, 7.5 % VAT on top as on every platform invoice (§ 1.4; § 10 item 3 is still open). `finance-admin` changes them in Console → Settings → SMS credit prices (settings `sms_credits.price_kobo`, `sms_credits.bundles`); a change applies to purchases started after it.
44. **How a bundle is paid — the platform payment path does not exist yet.** The docs put platform invoices, payments and GoProperty's Paystack in P06-02/P06-03, which are not built, so P05-03 builds their first slice in `Modules\SaasBilling` in the documented shape rather than a second path: a `platform_invoices` row (`kind` sms_credits, number `PR-2026-000123` from a platform counter), a `platform_payments` row (`PINV-{number}-a1`), GoProperty's own Paystack checkout (`services.paystack.secret_key`), the return page and `/webhooks/paystack`, both verified by fetch. Credits are added only on a confirmed payment, once per payment; a mismatch (amount, currency, mode) or a payment that arrives after the invoice was voided is **held** for platform staff (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 4.3 rule 2) — their review screen is P06-07. An abandoned checkout stays pending. No invoice PDF or receipt email yet (P06-02's platform invoice documents). Outside production only a Paystack **test** key (`sk_test_…`) is used; with none, bundles are not offered and support adds credits by adjustment.
45. **Staff adjustments:** `finance-admin` (`plans.manage`) adds or removes credits on the customer's console page with a reason, shown to the customer in the credit history and audited with the staff member; added credits never expire; removing never goes below zero (purchased credits first).
