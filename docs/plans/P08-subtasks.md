## P08-01 — customer dashboards

Started 2026-11-03 at the product owner's request ("Start P08-01"); one task, built by the orchestrator in the new module `Modules\Reports` (ARCHITECTURE.md § 5 "Reports (P08-01)").

### Defaults taken in P08-01 (orchestrator, 2026-11-03; for the product owner to confirm)

1. **One Reports page in the workspace sidebar** (`/o/{slug}/reports`) holds all six dashboards, for members holding `reports.view`: owners and administrators (already in the permission catalogue). Agents do not see it. Landlords keep their statements in `/owner` (P08-05); the agency's dashboards are not shown to them. No plan gates it: every plan has reports.
2. **Date range:** two Lagos days, both included, picked with a preset (This month — the default —, Last month, This quarter, This year, Last 12 months) or typed. At most 24 months; never after today (a later end becomes today); never before 2000; dates out of order are swapped; unreadable dates give this month. The building filter offers the active buildings, or all of them.
3. **Billed** = bills issued in the range (by the moment they were issued, not their due date or period), every kind: rent, utilities, and "other" (opening balances and other bills). **Void bills are left out.**
4. **Collected** = money that settled bills in the range, counted exactly as the owner statements count it (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 7.9, Payments' `OwnerCollections`): a confirmed payment's share of bills when it was confirmed, tenant credit when it was used for a bill, a voided receipt negative when it was voided. An overpayment held as credit is not collected until it settles a bill, so **credit refunded to a tenant is netted out** (it was never counted). Imported payment history counts when it was confirmed by the import, as in the owner statements.
5. **Collection rate** = collected ÷ billed over the same range, to a tenth of a percent, rounded down; "—" when nothing was billed. It is a cash measure: money collected in the range may be for bills issued earlier, so it can pass 100% (January 2027 in the test world: 1027.1%). A rate of "collected against the bills issued in the range" was not built.
6. **Arrears aging is as of today (Lagos), whatever the range:** bills still asking for money (unpaid, partly paid or overdue; void bills never) whose due date is today or earlier, by what each still owes. Age = days since the due date, **the due day itself being day 0**, in buckets 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ (91 days and more). Bills not yet due are shown beside the buckets as "Not yet due", outside the arrears. Balances in the past are not rebuilt (that needs payment history per day); the building filter applies. Tenant credit is not set against arrears (credit is applied to bills as they are issued).
7. **Upcoming renewals:** active leases whose last day is between today and 30, 60 or 90 days from today (90 by default, as the owner statements' renewals), soonest first, each with where its renewal stands (renewal drafted, rolls on by itself, not renewed yet). Not tied to the date range.
8. **Occupancy is now, not over the range:** units by status in active buildings (archived units and buildings left out), occupancy = occupied ÷ all units, rounded down, as on a building's page. Unit status history is not kept, so occupancy on past days is not shown.
9. **Expenses by category:** expenses paid in the range (by the day paid), archived ones left out, by category, largest first; spent on buildings and the organization's own (no building) in separate columns. The organization's own appear only when the report covers every building.
10. **Per-building performance:** one line per active building (and any other building with money in the range, e.g. archived, marked "no longer active"), by name: occupancy now, billed, collected, rate, owed past due today, expenses, and **collected less expenses**. The organization's own money linked to no building is a last line, "Not linked to a building", shown only for every building and only when it is not all zero.
11. **Exports:** each report has its own Excel/CSV export through the list export (keys `report_occupancy`, `report_collections`, `report_arrears`, `report_renewals`, `report_expenses`, `report_buildings`), with the page's filters; the arrears export lists every bill, the renewals export every lease (the page and the PDF list the first 20 and 40). The **PDF** is the whole page for the filters, in the agency's branding and layout as they are now, **rendered on each download and never kept** (a working document, not an issued one: no number, no verification code); it is throttled to 20 a minute per member and not audited (list exports are, as before).
12. **Names only:** the reports name tenants (arrears, renewals) but never show a phone, email, bank detail or payment reference, so they stay open in GoProperty's support sessions (read-only and write) and while the organization is suspended.
13. **A member narrowed to buildings** (an assigned agent given `reports.view` directly) sees only those buildings, never the organization's own money; asking for another building is dropped on the page and answers 404 on the PDF and the exports.
14. **Live sums for now:** the page works its figures out from the records on each load (a few queries per report, none per row). Caching them is P08-04.

## P08-02 — System Analytics dashboard

Started 2026-11-03 at the product owner's request ("Start P08-02"); one task, built by the orchestrator in `App\Platform\Analytics` (PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md § 3 "How it is built (P08-02)").

### Defaults taken in P08-02 (orchestrator, 2026-11-03; for the product owner to confirm)

15. **Customers are organizations that pay GoProperty:** platform-covered organizations (landlords, or anyone billed to a covered payer, BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 2) are counted apart as "platform-covered" and left out of revenue, MRR, customers by state and status, trial conversion, churn and the leaderboard. Units, tiers and usage count every organization still open (not cancelled), covered ones included. "Customers by state" means the Nigerian state on the organization's profile ("Not given" when empty).
16. **MRR** = for every customer whose subscription is active, payment overdue or in its final days (not trial, suspended or cancelled), its plan's monthly price at the terms in force today for its units now (metering's count: not deleted, in an active building), **before VAT**. A yearly subscription counts as a twelfth of its 10 months' price. ARR = 12 × MRR. Not taken from invoices (a yearly invoice would spike one month).
17. **Revenue this month vs last** = GoProperty's invoices issued in the Lagos month of the range's end (so far) and in the month before, **before VAT**, void invoices left out; subscription and SMS bundle invoices both count. Platform-covered invoices are not revenue.
18. **Collection rate of platform invoices** = money received in the range (confirmed payments into GoProperty's account) ÷ invoiced in the range, both VAT included, to a tenth of a percent, rounded down: a cash measure like P08-01's default 5, so it can pass 100%. Refunds are shown apart, not netted.
19. **Average days to pay** = over the invoices paid in the range (not covered ones), the Lagos days from issue to payment, averaged to a tenth of a day.
20. **Trial → paid** = first payments in the range (an organization's first confirmed payment of a subscription invoice; trials end into `active` by themselves, so payment is the conversion) ÷ trials that ended in the range. Over a short range the two need not be the same customers; a cohort view was not built.
21. **Churn** = paying customers whose subscription was cancelled in the range (from the subscription's audit entry, `subscription.cancelled`, whose previous state is not `trialing`) ÷ paying customers at the range's start (the stocks of the day before it, or its first day with stocks). Trials cancelled before paying are counted apart, not as churn. Suspensions are not churn.
22. **Active users** = customers' users (not staff) who **signed in** (password or passkey, `authentication_logs`) on the day, and in the 7 days ending it. Someone who stays signed in is not counted again; session activity is not tracked. The page shows the last day of the range and charts each day.
23. **Collections recorded** = agencies' confirmed tenant payments (`payments`, confirmed or later refunded, not deleted), by the Lagos day they were confirmed, by channel: bank statement match, Paystack (the agency's own), proof from the tenant, cash or POS, and a transfer recorded by a member. **Imported payment history is left out** (collected before GoProperty). GoProperty never handled this money.
24. **Leaderboard:** top 10 customers by units (on the range's last day), by units added over the range (last day against the day before the range; only growth above zero), and by **payment reliability** = subscription invoices due in the 365 days to the last day (due date passed, void ones left out) paid on or before their due date (Lagos), for customers with at least 3 such invoices, best first. Business names only; linked to the customer's page for staff with `customers.view` (analysts have none).
25. **The nightly summary** runs every hour at :10 and takes the Lagos day that ended at its first run after midnight (instead of once at 04:00, so a missed night is made up within the hour). Stocks are as they stand at that run. Days missed for longer get their **flows** back (up to 7 days, never before the first snapshot); their **stocks** cannot be rebuilt and stay empty (the page reads the nearest day that has them). A day is never rewritten after it is taken, except by hand (`php artisan metrics:snapshot <date>`): an invoice voided later stays counted on the day it was issued.
26. **Date range:** one for the whole page (Last 7, 30 — default —, 90 days, Last 12 months, This year, or two dates), ending yesterday at the latest, at most 731 days, never before 2020. Weeks start on Monday; months are Lagos months.
27. **Charts** are simple server-drawn bars (one series each, no script); every section downloads as CSV for the page's range, a row per day (the leaderboard and states as lists). Every download is audited (log `analytics`).
28. **Who:** `analytics.view` only (analyst, operations-manager, super-admin), as the permission catalogue already had it.

## P08-04 — Cached aggregates for dashboards

Built 2026-11-03 by a task agent in Core's `App\Dashboards` and `Modules\Reports` (ARCHITECTURE.md § 13 "How it is built (P08-04)"). No migration, no new table, no new package.

### Defaults taken in P08-04 (agent, 2026-11-03; for the product owner to confirm)

50. **Only the workspace's Reports page is cached** (the whole page and each of its six reports; its exports and PDF read the same entries). The workspace home shows no figures (the setup checklist only), so there is nothing there to cache.
51. **Owner statements are not cached** (`/owner/statements` and the agency's statement pages): they are the figures the agency marks Settled against, they cover one owner's buildings across organizations, and twelve months cost a few queries. Live figures are cheap there and must be exact.
52. **The console's System analytics is not cached:** it already reads only the nightly summary `daily_platform_metrics` (three queries a load), which is its cache. System operations (P08-03) and the delivery health widgets are live on purpose and are not cached.
53. **Five minutes** (ARCHITECTURE.md § 13), or until the organization's next relevant write, whichever comes first.
54. **Relevant writes** are every create, change or delete of a bill, payment, payment allocation, receipt, tenant credit entry, expense, building, unit, lease, tenant or import batch, and every building move (both organizations). A write that changes no figure (a credit refund, a bill's PDF stored) still renews the cache: it is simple and safe, and costs one cache write per committed transaction per organization. Writes are only read here; no state machine or writer behaves differently.
55. **One cache for everyone who sees the same buildings:** figures depend only on the organization, the buildings covered and the dates, never on who asks, so an owner and an administrator share an entry, and a narrowed member shares one with an owner who picked the same building. Who may open the page is checked before the cache is read; a member's narrowing is read on every load and never cached.
56. **"As of today" figures start again at Lagos midnight** (arrears aging, upcoming renewals, owed past due per building, the whole page): today's date is part of their key, so after midnight they are worked out again even when nothing was written. Collections and expenses depend only on their dates; occupancy only on writes.
57. **Exports and the PDF read the cached figures, like the page,** not a fresh read: every relevant write renews the cache, so it never holds figures older than the last relevant write, and the page, its exports and its PDF always show the same money.
58. **Support sessions and suspended organizations** read the same cache: reading figures is not a write, so nothing changes for them (P08-01 default 12).
59. **One entry per organization, report and buildings covered:** figures for other dates replace it, so the cache never fills with old date ranges (on shared hosting the database cache removes an expired row only when it is read again). Figures larger than 4 MB once deflated are not cached (worked out each time).
60. **Kept signed, read back as named classes only:** `cache.serializable_classes` stays false; the figures are a serialized string signed with the application key and read back allowing only the report's own classes.
61. **A read replica is optional (VPS):** `DB_REPORTS_HOST` (and `_PORT`, `_USERNAME`, `_PASSWORD`, each falling back to the main connection's) sends the reports' reads to a replica, writes always to the main database. Unset (shared hosting, the tests), the reports read the main database.
62. **After a write, the main database for 60 seconds** (`DB_REPORTS_PRIMARY_AFTER_WRITE`): an organization's reports read the main database for that long after its last relevant write, so a lagging replica never fills the cache with figures from before the write. A replica that lags by more than that is a hosting problem to fix, not something the reports work around.
63. **An unreachable cache never fails anything:** writes still commit (the cached figures then live out their five minutes) and pages work their figures out live; the error is reported.
64. **No page shows when the figures were worked out** ("as of 10:02"): they are at most five minutes behind a write the cache missed, which should not happen; a label was not built.
