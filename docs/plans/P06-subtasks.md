# P06 — SaaS billing, compliance monitoring & full platform console: tasks and defaults

Rules: [BILLING_PAYMENTS.md](../../BILLING_PAYMENTS.md) § 1 (plans, prices, features, 30 days' notice), § 2 (subscription lifecycle), § 3 (metering), § 4–6 (Paystack, bank transfer, dunning); [DATABASE_SCHEMA.md § 2](../../DATABASE_SCHEMA.md); [ARCHITECTURE.md § 4](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) (suspended organizations are read-only), § 6.10 (feature enablement); [PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md § 1A](../../PLATFORM_CONSOLE.md) (plans are `finance-admin`'s, audited, customers told 30 days ahead); AGENT.md § 3 rules 2 (kobo), 5 (state machines through their services) and 6 (idempotency).

## P06-01 — plans, features and subscriptions

Started 2026-10-30 at the product owner's request ("start P06-01"). Split in two (IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md § 3.1, "Changing scope"):

- **P06-01a — plans in the database.** The launch plans of § 1.1–1.2 move from `config/plans.php` into tables, with versioned terms, the 30-day rule, the customers' notice and the console's plan editor. Everything that read the config (landing page, signup, SMS allowance, handover quote, import report) reads the tables.
- **P06-01b — subscriptions and plan gates.** `subscriptions` and `SubscriptionService` (every lifecycle state of § 2), the trial, Settings → Subscription, and the gates: features and limits enforced by middleware (HTTP and Livewire) and again in the services.

### Defaults (orchestrator, 2026-10-30; for the product owner to confirm)

1. **Where it lives: Core, not `Modules\SaasBilling`.** Plans, features, overrides and subscriptions are read by Core (signup, landing page, SMS allowance, branding, the member limit, the write guard) and by the console, and Core may not use a module's internals. So they live in `App\Plans` and `App\Subscriptions` (as SMS credits did in P05-03), the console's editing in `App\Platform\Plans`. SaasBilling keeps invoices, payments and Paystack, and builds metering (P06-02) and dunning (P06-05) on Core's `SubscriptionService`.
2. **A plan is one row; its priced terms are versions.** `plans` holds what never changes a bill: key, name, audience, the landing page's lines, featured, custom ("from" pricing), trial days, offered to new signups, order. `plan_versions` holds the terms: base price, included units, extra-unit price, and its features and limits (`plan_feature`, one row per feature per version). Annual billing stays "pay 10 months, get 12" for every plan (`config/plans.php`, `months_paid_per_year`).
3. **The 30-day rule.** A new version of a plan that anyone is on (a subscription that is not cancelled) takes effect on a Lagos date at least **30 days after it is saved**; saving it is the notice. The editor proposes the first 1st of a month that is 30 days away (the billing day, § 3). A plan nobody is on may change from today. Name, audience, landing lines, featured, order, "offered to new signups" and trial days change at once (audited): they change nothing anyone pays, and trial days only affect trials that start later.
4. **Which terms a customer has.** A subscription follows its plan's version in effect on the first day of its current billing period, or today while it has no period (in trial). So existing customers keep their price until their next billing period starts on or after the version's date ("until their next renewal", § 1.3): monthly customers from that 1st, annual customers from their next yearly renewal. Grandfathering one customer on old terms comes with the console's subscription pages (P06-07).
5. **The notice.** Saving a version with notice emails the owners of every organization on that plan (template `plans.terms_changing`, transactional: price before and after, the date, what changes), once per organization and version (`plan_change_notices`), queued in chunks on `notifications`. Owners and administrators also see a banner on every workspace page and on Settings → Subscription until the date. A scheduled version may be cancelled until its date (audited); nobody is emailed about the cancellation and the banner goes.
6. **Feature registry** (`App\Plans\Feature`, launch matrix of § 1.2): switches `own_paystack`, `whatsapp`, `building_group` (Growth and up), `agent_assignment`, `approval_workflows`, `remove_powered_by`, `assisted_import` (Scale and up); limits `staff_users` (3, 10, unlimited, unlimited) and `sms_credits` (100, 500, 2,000, 2,000 a month). A per-organization override (`organization_feature_overrides`: on/off or another limit, with reason, staff member and optional expiry) wins over the plan; the console's screen for overrides is P06-07's.
7. **Gates enforced in P06-01b:** `staff_users` (inviting a member and accepting an invitation; members and waiting invitations both count), `own_paystack` (connecting Paystack, and tenants' "Pay online" when the plan lacks it: the saved connection is kept), `whatsapp` (replaces `services.whatsapp.plans`), `remove_powered_by` (replaces `branding.remove_footer_plans`) and `sms_credits` (the monthly allowance, from the plan instead of `config/plans.php`). **Not gated yet:** `agent_assignment` (gating it would change what agents of a Starter or Growth agency can see, a tenancy change that needs the product owner's word, AGENT.md § 8), `approval_workflows` (no approval workflow exists yet), `building_group` (P07), `assisted_import` (done by GoProperty staff).
8. **Over a limit after a plan change or an override.** Nothing is removed: members stay, but no one new is invited until the organization is under the limit. A customer may not move itself to a plan whose limits it is over (it is told what to remove first); staff may (P06-07).
9. **Trial.** Choosing a plan in the signup wizard starts a `trialing` subscription on that plan, ending on `organizations.trial_ends_at` (14 days, the plan's trial days). Changing plan during the trial keeps its end date. A trial has the chosen plan's features and limits, so nothing switches off when it ends. Organizations that chose a plan before P06-01 get their subscription from a migration. An organization with no subscription and no plan (the wizard has not reached its plan step) is not gated.
10. **When the trial ends** a job (`subscriptions-trials`, hourly at :15, `billing` queue, so a missed run is made up) makes the subscription `active` with its first period starting that day, through `SubscriptionService`. P06-02 issues the first invoice at that point (it listens for `SubscriptionStatusChanged`).
11. **Lifecycle (§ 2).** `trialing → active | cancelled`; `active → past_due | cancelled`; `past_due → active | grace`; `grace → active | suspended`; `suspended → active | cancelled`; `cancelled → active` (reactivated and paid, within the 90 days data is kept). Each change is one audit entry (log `subscriptions`) and one `SubscriptionStatusChanged` event. `suspended` and `cancelled` also suspend or close the organization (its `status`, read-only for members, ARCHITECTURE.md § 4); `active` again restores the status it had, **only when the subscription was what suspended it**: a staff suspension is lifted by staff. The ladder that moves subscriptions along (failed charge, grace, suspension) is P06-05's.
12. **Customer's plan change** (Settings → Subscription, owners with `organization.billing`): takes effect at once at the new plan's current terms; the current invoice is untouched (§ 3, no proration). Not while the organization is suspended or cancelled (only paying is allowed then).

## P06-02 — metering, platform invoices and the VAT line

Started 2026-10-30 at the product owner's request ("start P06-02"). Built in `Modules\SaasBilling` (default 1): `Metering\SubscriptionMetering`, `Jobs\MeterUnits`, `Listeners\InvoiceWhenActive`, `Contracts\SubscriptionInvoices`.

### Decided by the product owner (2026-10-30)

- **The first part month is charged by days.** Monthly billing periods are calendar months, billed on the 1st. When a trial ends after the 1st (say 16 November), the first invoice is issued that day for 16–30 November at 15/30 of the month's price, units counted that day; a full month follows on each 1st.
- **Yearly subscriptions are billed once a year.** Invoiced when the trial ends for 12 months at 10 months' price ("pay 10 months, get 12"), for the units counted that day, then on the same date each year. Units added during the year count at the next renewal.

### Defaults taken in P06-02 (for the product owner to confirm)

13. **Part months.** A part month costs the month's price (base + extra units × unit price) × days charged ÷ days in that month, rounded to the nearest kobo, halves up; VAT is worked out on that subtotal. The same applies when a customer is active again after a lapse (below).
14. **Which subscriptions are billed:** `active` and `past_due` (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 3 step 1). Trials, `grace`, `suspended` and `cancelled` are not. A customer is never billed for a whole month (a whole year, for yearly) it spent suspended or cancelled: when it is active again its next period starts that day, charged by days. A customer that pays within the month it lapsed in is billed from where it left off. `cancel_at_period_end` (nothing sets it yet) stops billing after the current period.
15. **Terms and count.** An invoice uses the plan's version in effect on the period's first day (default 4) and the units counted when it is made (§ 1.4's rule: every unit not archived in a building not archived, whatever its status). It keeps the plan version and name, base price, included units, extra units and their price, the months or days charged and the VAT rate, so it explains itself after the plan changes. Changing plan mid-period changes the next invoice only (§ 3).
16. **When.** `saas-meter-units` runs **hourly at :45** on `billing` (ARCHITECTURE.md § 7 said once, at 03:00 on the 1st): a missed run is made up within the hour and yearly renewals fall on any day; a monthly invoice is issued in the first hour of the 1st. A subscription is also invoiced at once when it becomes `active` (the trial ends; a payment after a lapse), by a listener on `SubscriptionStatusChanged`. Both bill only a period that is due and not yet invoiced: the invoice is unique per subscription and period, written under the subscription's lock.
17. **VAT and "BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 10".** The acceptance cites § 10, which lists the open business decisions; its item 3 (whether GoProperty is VAT-registered, which decides the compliance lines such as a VAT or tax number) is still open. P06-02 reads the acceptance as § 1.4's rule: VAT at `config('plans.vat_percent')` (7.5 %) on every subscription invoice, as its own line, on the subtotal, rounded to the nearest kobo (halves up), the rate kept on each invoice so a later change applies to later invoices only. No registration number is printed until § 10.3 is answered.
18. **Who is told.** The organization's **owners** (§ 3 step 4: not administrators), in the app and by email when they have an address (template `billing.invoice_issued`, transactional, GoProperty's branding), and the owners of its payer when that is another organization. Due 7 days after issue (§ 3, net 7).
19. **Platform-covered organizations** (an organization or payer with `platform_covered`, § 2): the invoice is issued as **paid** at once with the note "Covered by GoProperty", audited (`platform_invoice.covered`), and nobody is asked to pay.
20. **The metering ledger** (`subscription_unit_events`) has one row per billed period: the units counted, the included units and the units paid for, and why it differs from the row before (`initial`, `plan_change`, `unit_added`, `unit_removed`, `unchanged`); `platform_invoice_id` replaces the first schema's `source_bill_id`. Units added and removed between billing days are in the Properties audit log, not this ledger.
21. **No invoice PDF yet.** The invoice email and Settings → Subscription show every line (base, extra units, subtotal, VAT, total). A PDF in GoProperty's branding (with `theme.legal_name` and `theme.address`, and § 10.3's registration lines once decided) is a follow-up, with paying it (P06-03, P06-04).
22. **`subscriptions.payer_organization_id`** is added (null = the organization pays) and read by metering; nothing sets it yet (staff, P06-07).

## P06-03 — paying subscription invoices through GoProperty's Paystack

Started 2026-10-30 at the product owner's request ("start P06-03"). Built in `Modules\SaasBilling\Payments` on P05-03's Paystack client and webhook.

### Decided by the product owner (2026-10-30)

- **No stored cards and no auto-charge yet.** Owners pay each invoice through Paystack's checkout; GoProperty keeps no card authorization. The plan row's "stored authorization, auto-charge" (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 3 step 5, § 4.1) is set aside until the product owner asks for it.
- **The sandbox acceptance is proven with Paystack simulated** by its documented test-mode answers in automated tests (no test key here, and a checkout needs a person); a real test-mode payment is done once GoProperty's test key is set (PRE-01).

### Defaults taken in P06-03 (for the product owner to confirm)

23. **One settlement for every GoProperty payment.** `Payments\PlatformPaymentSettlement` settles SMS bundles (P05-03) and subscription invoices alike, from the return, the webhook, a replay and the reconciliation: verify by fetch, exactly the total in naira in the account's mode for an open invoice, else held for staff; once per payment under its lock.
24. **Each Pay is its own attempt** (`PINV-{number}-a1`, `-a2`, …). Before a new one, the invoice's pending attempts are asked about, so an owner who paid and never came back is not charged twice; an invoice already paid refuses a new attempt. A second payment that still arrives for a paid invoice is held for staff (refunds: P06-07).
25. **A failed charge** (Paystack's `charge.failed`, verified) fails that attempt and leaves the subscription invoice **open**, to be paid again (an SMS bundle's invoice is voided as before). Retries and failure emails are the dunning ladder's (P06-05).
26. **Reactivation (§ 6):** a confirmed payment makes a `past_due`, `grace` or `suspended` subscription active again (restoring a suspended organization) once no other subscription invoice is overdue. A cancelled subscription is reactivated by staff (P06-07).
27. **Receipt:** the owners are told in the app and by email (template `billing.payment_received`: amount, date, invoice, period, Paystack reference). No receipt PDF yet (with the invoice PDF, default 21).
28. **Reconciliation:** `saas-paystack-reconcile`, hourly at :50 on `billing`, asks Paystack about every payment still pending after 15 minutes, up to 72 hours old (§ 4.2 said "open invoices older than 1h"); an abandoned checkout older than that stays pending and is no longer asked about.
29. **Who pays:** owners (`organization.billing`), from Settings → Subscription; also while the organization is suspended (paying is how it becomes active again, § 2); never GoProperty staff while impersonating. Checkout offers card, bank, USSD and bank transfer.
30. **Console → Operations → Paystack webhooks** shows the calls to GoProperty's own account only (agencies' own accounts stay in their organizations); seen with `operations.view`, `invoices.manage` or `webhooks.replay`; **Replay** with `webhooks.replay` (technical-admin), only a signed call, audited (log `webhooks`). A replay asks Paystack again and never settles a payment twice.

## P06-04 — bank transfers to GoProperty: proofs and staff confirmation

Started 2026-10-31 at the product owner's request ("Start P06-04"). Built in `Modules\SaasBilling\Payments` (`TransferProofs`, `TransferReviews`, `Reactivation`) with Core's Settings → Subscription and two console pages.

### Decided by the product owner (2026-10-31)

- **GoProperty's subscription invoices are paid in full** (default 35 confirmed): a bank transfer short of the total is rejected with the reason. **Part payments in installments are for tenants' rent bills**, where the agency or agent decides the number of installments. Tenants can already pay a bill in several payments (status `partially_paid`, P03-10a); a limit or schedule of installments set by the agency is not built yet and is planned as its own task.

### Defaults taken in P06-04 (for the product owner to confirm)

31. **GoProperty's account is set in the console**, Settings → **Bank transfer details** (`plans.manage`, finance-admin): bank, account name and 10-digit account number (settings `billing.transfer_bank`, `billing.transfer_account_name`, `billing.transfer_account_number`, audited by the settings store). All three or none. Until they are set, no invoice offers a bank transfer.
32. **Where and who.** Each open subscription invoice on Settings → Subscription shows the account and the invoice number as the narration (§ 5 step 1), and so does the invoice email. Owners (`organization.billing`) send the proof there, under the same rules as Pay (default 29): also while the organization is suspended (Livewire's upload steps are allowed on that page for it), never while staff impersonate. Administrators see the account but send nothing. SMS credit bundles are still paid by Paystack only.
33. **The proof**: a file (JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, at most 10 MB; media collection `platform_payment_proofs`, evidence: never deleted), the amount sent (starts as the total), the day (in the last 90 days, not in the future), the name on the sending account, and optionally the bank and the narration used. It is a `platform_payments` row, method `bank_transfer`, status `pending`, asking the invoice's total. **The invoice stays open and the subscription as it was** (the acceptance). One waiting proof per invoice: another is refused until staff review it. At most 5 proofs per organization per day. The same file sent before by the organization is flagged for staff, never refused.
34. **Review** in Console → Billing → **Bank transfers**: waiting proofs first (a count on the menu), with the organization, invoice, total and status, what the owner says was sent (in red when it differs from the total), from whom, the narration, "sent before" for a repeated file, and **Open proof** (a signed link valid 15 minutes). Seen with `payments.confirm_transfer` or `invoices.manage`; Confirm and Reject only with `payments.confirm_transfer` (finance-admin, super-admin). No email to staff when a proof arrives.
35. **Confirm** (confirmed by the product owner, 2026-10-31) means the invoice's **whole total** reached GoProperty's account; the staff member ticks that they checked. The payment is confirmed (who and when), the invoice paid, a lapsed subscription active again by the same rule as Paystack (default 26, now `Payments\Reactivation` for both), one audit entry `platform_invoice.paid` with the staff member as causer and method `bank_transfer`, and the owners get the usual receipt (`billing.payment_received`, reference "Bank transfer"). Refused when the invoice is no longer open (paid by card meanwhile: reject the proof; refunds are P06-07's). **No part payments**: a short transfer is rejected with the reason.
36. **Reject** needs a reason (3–500 characters). The payment becomes `rejected` (kept, never changed), the invoice stays open, audited `platform_payment.transfer_rejected`, and the owners are told in the app and by email (template `billing.transfer_rejected`) with the reason; they may send another proof.
37. **Nothing confirms a proof automatically** (§ 5 step 4); Paystack's dedicated virtual accounts (§ 4.1) remain the way to match transfers automatically later. The receipt template's line "Paystack reference" now reads "Reference" (it also serves transfers); installs that already seeded the template keep their text until a content manager edits it.

## P06-05 — dunning ladder and suspension gating

Started 2026-10-31 at the product owner's request ("start P06-05"). Built in `Modules\SaasBilling\Dunning` (`DunningLadder`, `DunningTemplates`), `Jobs\RunDunning` and Core's `App\Subscriptions\DunningSchedule`; the suspension gating itself is the central write guard of P01-06.

### Decided by the product owner (2026-10-31)

- **A failed payment is not retried.** No card is kept (2026-10-30), so § 6's "+1d, +3d, +5d" retries have nothing to charge. Each payment Paystack fails emails the owners once, with a Pay now link; after that the due-date ladder does the chasing.
- **GoProperty pays for its own billing SMS.** The SMS on suspension and every 14 days after take no credits from the agency, so they still go when it has none; only to owners whose phone is verified.

### Defaults taken in P06-05 (for the product owner to confirm)

38. **Each step counts from the one before** (Lagos days): `past_due` the day after the due date; `grace` 7 days after `past_due`; `suspended` 7 days after `grace`; a reminder every 14 days suspended; `cancelled` 90 days after suspension. With the hourly job these are § 6's "7 days" and "14 days past due"; after an outage the customer still gets every day of grace and every warning (one step a run). After an outage only the latest reminder due is sent.
39. **Who is told:** the owners of the organization and of its payer (as invoices are sent, default 18), not administrators; administrators see the banner. In the app and by email at every step; the grace warning once a day (7 in all, the first on the day grace starts); SMS only on suspension and its reminders (§ 6), to verified phones.
40. **The notices are templates** (content-manager edits them): `billing.payment_failed`, `billing.past_due`, `billing.grace_warning`, `billing.suspended`, `billing.suspension_reminder`, `billing.cancelled`, and the SMS `billing.suspended.sms`, `billing.suspension_reminder.sms` (one page, no link: "Settings > Subscription"). Each email has Pay now to Settings → Subscription and names the day of the next step.
41. **Once per step and day**: a `dunning_notices` row (unique per subscription, step and day) written in the transaction that moves the subscription, under the organization's and subscription's locks; `saas-dunning` hourly at :55 on `billing`.
42. **Cancellation after 90 days suspended** closes the organization (read-only, data kept); reactivating it is staff's (P06-07); the owners are told by email and in the app, not by SMS.
43. **The banner escalates:** past due and grace say the day the organization would be suspended; once the subscription suspended it, owners and administrators see "pay the open invoices and everything works again at once" with the day it would be cancelled. Agents see only the suspension notice.
44. **Self-healing:** a past-due, grace or suspended subscription with nothing overdue (two invoices paid at the same moment, each seeing the other open) is made active by the ladder. Platform-covered organizations and payers are never dunned (§ 2).
45. **The ten write actions** proven refused while suspended, and working again once paid, over HTTP as the browser sends them: add a building, add a unit, add a tenant, draft a lease, activate a lease, record a payment, void a bill, raise a ticket, invite a member, save the organization profile. The guard itself covers every Livewire call and non-GET request (P01-06).

## P06-06 — management handover on live subscriptions

Started 2026-10-31 at the product owner's request ("Let start P06-06"). The payer switch first planned here stays dropped (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 7.8): no organization pays another's bill. Built in SaasBilling's `Quotes\MonthlyBills` (with `Metering\BillingPeriod::next()` and `Contracts\NextInvoice`) and the Handovers accept screen; no migration, package, permission or queue.

### Defaults taken in P06-06 (for the product owner to confirm)

46. **The next billing day is the subscription's own:** the trial's last day while in trial (that first invoice is charged by days to the month's end, default 13), the 1st for monthly subscriptions, the renewal date for yearly ones, today when a period is already due. A grace or suspended organization is shown the period it would be billed once active again. An organization with no subscription is shown the 1st of next month on its signup plan, else the cheapest plan for the units.
47. **The screen's amounts are the next invoice's**, worked out by metering's own rules for the units after the handover: the plan's version in effect on that period's first day (a scheduled price included), the period, how it is charged, the subtotal before and after, VAT and the total. The headline keeps § 7.3's monthly wording at those terms; the next invoice is stated under it. A platform-covered organization is told it has nothing to pay.
48. **The consent amount stays the monthly price after** (`monthly_after_kobo`): acceptance is refused when it changed while the screen was open. The next billing day and the next invoice's subtotal before and after go in the acceptance's audit entry; no new columns.
49. **No proration and no re-issue** (§ 1.4): accepting, ending or returning invoices and credits nothing. Each organization counts the units it has on its own billing day, so the receiving organization pays for the buildings from its next billing day and the landlord's organization stops on its next billing day; a building that moves twice between two billing days is billed by whoever has it on each one's day. Proration waits for the post-MVP refinement.


## P06-07 — the platform console's full build

Started 2026-10-31 at the product owner's request ("start P06-07"). The row lists eight console areas; customers (P01-06), plans (P06-01a), bank transfers (P06-04), the Paystack webhook log (P06-03), staff and approvals (P01-07) already exist. Split in three (IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md § 3.1, "Changing scope"):

- **P06-07a — customers' subscriptions and feature overrides** (Core, `App\Platform\Customers`, `App\Platform\Plans`): the subscription on the organization's page, staff plan changes, cancelling and reactivating a subscription, feature overrides.
- **P06-07b — invoices, payments and refunds** (SaasBilling with `App\Platform\Billing`): Billing → Invoices and Billing → Payments across organizations, resending an invoice email, refunds with the two-person approval above the configured amount.
- **P06-07c — the audit-log, access-log and staff-audit viewers** (`App\Platform`): the audit log across organizations, the authentication log of both guards, and each staff member's actions.

The acceptance ("every page policy-checked per staff role") is proven for every console page old and new by one test that opens each page as each default role.

### Defaults taken in P06-07 (for the product owner to confirm)

50. **Staff change a customer's plan** on the organization's page (`customers.change_plan`: operations-manager, super-admin): any plan, custom ones and plans no longer offered included, and either billing cycle, at once at the plan's current terms, through `SubscriptionService::changePlan` (no limit check: an organization over a limit keeps everything and adds nothing until it is under it, default 8). A reason is required and kept in the audit entry; the current invoice is untouched (§ 3); the owners are not emailed (staff change a plan at the customer's request; Settings → Subscription and the next invoice show it).
51. **Not built in P06-07:** keeping one customer on old terms (pinned terms would have to reach metering and the handover quote; a custom plan does it today), extending a grace period (`customers.extend_grace` stays unused), `cancel_at_period_end`, and setting a payer (P06-06 dropped organization-to-organization payers; platform cover is set at signup).
52. **Cancel and reactivate a subscription** (`customers.suspend`: operations-manager, super-admin, who already suspend and reactivate organizations). **Cancel subscription** (any status but cancelled) through `SubscriptionService::cancel`: the organization is closed (read-only, data kept). **Reactivate subscription** only for a cancelled one, through `SubscriptionService::activate`: the organization reopens when the subscription closed it, and metering invoices a period from that day, charged by days (P06-02), which the dunning ladder then follows. Both take a reason (audit only, as for suspensions) and an optional note quoted in the email to the owners, sent after the commit.
53. **Feature overrides** on the organization's page, a new permission `customers.override_features` (operations-manager, super-admin): every feature with the plan's value, the override in force (on or off, or a limit, empty = unlimited; reason; who; until) and what applies. **Set override** (feature, value, reason 3–500 characters, optional end date in the future) replaces the feature's override; **End override** sets its end to now (the row is kept). Only `App\Platform\Plans\FeatureOverrides` writes them, re-checking the permission, each change one audit entry (log `customers`, `feature_override.set` / `feature_override.ended`, the staff member as causer, before and after). `sms_credits` is not offered: the page's SMS allowance already sets it. Owners are not told; an override that reaches its end stops applying by itself.
54. **The subscription on the organization's page:** plan and cycle, status and when it changed, trial end, current period, units last billed, platform-covered; the customers list gains the subscription's plan and status (column and filter).
55. **Billing → Invoices** (`invoices.manage` or `emails.resend`): every GoProperty invoice (subscriptions and SMS bundles) across organizations, newest first, filters for status, kind, overdue and organization; an invoice's page with its lines, payments and refunds. **Send invoice email again** (`emails.resend` or `invoices.manage`) emails the owners the issued invoice once more (at most 3 times an hour per invoice), audited. Invoices are never edited or voided here (credit notes are post-MVP, BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 8).
56. **Billing → Payments** (`invoices.manage`, `payments.confirm_transfer` or `refunds.create`): every payment into GoProperty's account, Paystack and bank transfer, held ones counted on the menu; filters for method, status and organization; a payment's page with its refunds.
57. **Refunds** (`refunds.create`: finance-admin, super-admin) of a confirmed or held payment, the whole amount or part of what is not refunded yet, with a reason. Paystack payments are refunded through Paystack's refund API; a bank transfer is refunded by GoProperty's own transfer and recorded with its reference. **Above the configured amount** (setting `billing.refund_approval_above_kobo`, default ₦100,000, Console → Settings → Refunds, new permission `refunds.configure`, super-admin only) a refund waits for a second staff member with `refunds.approve` (never the requester; 7 days); at or below it, it runs at once. The amount counts the payment's earlier refunds (refunded, being refunded or waiting), so splitting a refund never avoids the approval. A rejected or expired request refunds nothing.
58. **What a refund changes:** refunding the **whole** payment that paid a subscription invoice reopens the invoice (BILLING_PAYMENTS.md § 8), due 7 days after the refund, and the dunning ladder follows; a **part** refund leaves it paid (goodwill, the reason in the audit log). A held payment's refund and an SMS bundle's refund change no invoice and no credits (staff remove credits with Adjust SMS credits if needed). The owners are told by email (template `billing.refund_issued`).
59. **Paystack refunds are asynchronous:** a refund Paystack accepts is `processing` and its effects apply at once; Paystack's `refund.processed` and `refund.failed` webhooks (and **Check with Paystack** on the refund) move it to `processed` or `failed`; a failed refund is shown in red for staff to settle by hand (its effects are not undone automatically).
60. **The audit log** (`audit.view`, super-admin by default): every entry across organizations, newest first, filters for organization, log, event, who did it (customer, staff, system), staff member, during a support session and dates; an entry's page with what changed, IP, user agent, reason and its link in the hash chain. Read-only, no export. Each opening of the list and of an entry is itself an audit entry (`audit.viewed`, log `audit`).
61. **The access log** (new permission `access_log.view`, area Audit: super-admin and technical-admin): the authentication log of customers and staff, newest first, filters for account type, event, dates, new devices and IP, and a search by email (matched to accounts and to the hashed address of failed attempts). Viewing it is audited (`access_log.viewed`).
62. **Staff audit:** a staff member's profile gains **Actions** (entries they caused, `audit.view`), beside the existing history about them; the audit log filters by staff member.
