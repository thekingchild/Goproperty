# Sub-task plan: P02 and P03

Companion to [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md § 3](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) (P02, P03 rows) and the ledger in [docs/PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). Drafted 2026-10-26 while P01 was being built; re-check it against what P01 actually delivered before starting P02.

Each row is one agent session in its own git worktree. A worktree is cut from `main` only after the previous wave has merged and `composer ci` is green. Run at most two sub-tasks at once. Sub-IDs go into `docs/PROGRESS.md` as § 3.1 describes, and the parent ID is marked Complete only when all of its sub-IDs are done. Every original acceptance criterion (AC) maps to exactly one sub-task, marked **AC**; rows marked "Sub" are extra checks.

Some files are shared and cause merge conflicts: `composer.json`/`composer.lock`, `modules_statuses.json`, `routes/web.php`, `bootstrap/app.php`, `phpunit.xml`, `phpstan.neon`, `tests/Pest.php` and `resources/views/layouts/app/sidebar.blade.php`. The waves below let at most one sub-task per wave edit any of them. A new migration may only add a foreign key to a table that is already on `main`.

**What exists today (before P01).** No `Modules/` folder yet; Core lives in `app/`. `phpunit.xml`, `tests/Pest.php` and `phpstan.neon` do not look at `Modules/`, and the module-boundary test in `tests/Feature/Core/ModuleSkeletonTest.php` is skipped. These packages are not installed: laravel-medialibrary and simple-excel (spatie/laravel-permission arrives with P01-01). A QR library (bacon) is already in the lock file through Fortify. `/pay` and `/verify` are placeholder routes that return 404.

**Settled by the product owner (2026-10-27, during P03):**
- **Statement matches become pending payments (P03-07b).** Agents never see bank statements (P03-07a). When a statement's credit line matches a bill (high confidence: the bill's payment reference in the narration; or a suggested match: amount plus a similar name), P03-07b records a payment waiting for confirmation (source `statement_match`, linked to the line, `PaymentRecorder` key `statement-line:{id}`). It appears in the existing payments confirmation queue, narrowed by tenant like proof uploads, showing only that line's date, amount and narration; agents confirm or reject it with `payments.confirm`. Owners and administrators may bulk-confirm high-confidence matches. Unmatched lines and splitting one credit across bills stay with owners/administrators on the statement page.
- **Tenant credit at a handover is settled per tenant (P03-14).** Credit is money the tenant paid into the landlord's account, so it never silently moves. The accept screen lists each moving tenant's unused credit (and unpaid bills, which stay with the landlord as history). For each tenant the landlord chooses: **pass to the agency** (default) — paid outside GoProperty; the agency records it as the tenant's opening credit only after confirming the money arrived — or **refund the tenant** — paid outside GoProperty and recorded as a refund that closes the credit. Until settled, the credit stays on the landlord's books as owed to the tenant, visible to both organizations, never lost or counted twice.

**Settled by the product owner (2026-10-28, after P03-14):**
- **A return is one-way.** When an agency returns buildings it holds without a handover (added or imported by the agency), the landlord's owner accepts, the buildings move into the landlord's organization and the handover ends as `returned`: final, no notice, no end date, nothing moves back. If the agency should keep managing them, the landlord then offers them with a normal handover. A landlord without an organization registers a free `property_owner` one first. History stays with the agency; the landlord's owner link there is removed. Built in P04-04.
- **Open bills stay with the landlord, who collects them** (as BILLING § 7.4 says). Safeguard: the landlord's record of a moved tenant cannot be archived while it has unpaid bills or unsettled credit, so the tenant keeps the page where they pay what they still owe. Built in P04-04.
- **Tenants are copied (or matched by phone), never moved,** so the landlord keeps their history. The landlord's record shows "Moved to {agency} on {date}" and archives itself once nothing is owed either way; on a return the tenant's original record (recorded on the handover) is used first, then the phone, so no third record is made. Built in P04-04.
- **Owners see tenant names only** by default (privacy, NDPA; agencies keep the tenant relationship). Later the agency may switch on "share tenant contact details" per owner. A landlord managing their own buildings is an `org-owner` there and sees everything. Built in P08-05.
- **What the landlord sees in `/owner`** (added by the product owner): upcoming renewals of tenants, billing, utilities paid, rent paid, and the total the agency owes the landlord for each month, marked Settled or Unsettled by the agency. GoProperty only records the settlement; money is paid outside it. Built in P08-05, which brings the MVP owner statement forward and takes "net due to owner" and the settled mark from post-launch item 3 (Owner payouts); payout approvals stay post-launch. Defaults to confirm: commission is a percentage of rent collected, set by the agency per owner profile; utilities paid are shown but count toward the amount due only when the agency says utilities pass to the landlord; only expenses the agency marks "charged to owner" are deducted; a payment confirmed after a month is settled counts in the next month, never changing the settled one.
